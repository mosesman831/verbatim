# v4.5 measured report — I1–I6 measured experiments (D01–D12)

Generated by `python -m eval.v45.run --experiment all --out eval/v45/out`.
Every number below is measured against disposable real `Store` instances —
claims consolidated through `consolidate_windowed`, views through
`Synthesizer.compose`, branches through `BranchService` + the real
lifecycle/review machinery, deliveries through
`verbatim/procedures/transfer.py`, refresh jobs through the real `JobQueue` →
`handle_consolidate` path, recall through `recall_v3`. Nothing is simulated;
declared ground truths are labeled as such.

**Reading this report.** The D-scenario acceptance gates are measured at
harness scale. Per SPEC_V4_5 §17, host qualification, statistical
superiority, and independent reproduction remain separate statuses — the
published disposition table (`eval/v45/dispositions.json`) correctly keeps
I1–I6 at `defer`/`unmeasured` until those higher bars are met, and D24's
test pin enforces it.

## I2 — repair-local recomputation vs full rebuild (D03, D04)

Corpus: 16 independent claim slots (one structured claim each), one real
`consolidate_windowed` observation per slot, one persisted
`Synthesizer.compose` view per claim. One localized correction (c0 rev1
closed, rev2 opened). Paired arms in identical seeded stores — the rebuild
arm receives the *same* correction (V45-04.04's same-input requirement).
Full data: `eval/v45/out/i2_repair_report.{json,md}`.

| metric | localized repair | full rebuild | reduction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| objects scanned | **2** | 65 | **96.9%** |
| objects evaluated | **2** | 32 | **93.8%** |
| objects recomputed | **3** | 18 | **83.3%** |

- **D03 — met.** The repair arm recomputes **83.3% fewer** objects than the
  same-input full rebuild (≥50% required by V45-04.04), at equal
  post-state: the corrected observation text is live on both arms and the
  live observation sets are identical. Invalidation is immediate —
  `invalidate_dependents` bumped the scope epoch and held 1 view + 1
  observation *inside the correction's own commit*, so no stale readable
  view survives the correction (V45-04.02).
- **D04 — met.** Both incomplete-plan fences refused with
  `CONTEXT_INCOMPLETE` and committed nothing: a declared forecast that
  omitted the real dependents (2 missing: the slot's observation + bound
  view), and a plan whose closure grew between plan and apply (a new view
  bound the same pinned revision — the apply-time re-walk caught it; the
  late view stayed `active`, never recomputed nor marked).
- Producer dispatch measured: `consolidate_windowed` re-derived only the
  touched slot (2 candidates evaluated vs 32 scope-wide), and the held
  view recomposed through the real `compose` path into a successor object
  binding c0 rev2.

## I5 — reversible memory branches (D09, D10)

Six scenarios, each in its own disposable store, every gate measured from
the typed error the real path returns plus committed post-state.
Full data: `eval/v45/out/i5_branch_report.{json,md}`.

| scenario | measured outcome | met |
| --- | --- | --- |
| d09 isolation + reviewed apply | claim visible to `recall_v3` before apply; apply before review → `INVALID_TRANSITION`; after submit+apply head = `archived`, recall drops it; replay → `replayed=True` | yes |
| moved parent fencing | submit → `STALE_PROPOSAL`; after rebase+submit, second move → apply `STALE_PROPOSAL` | yes |
| d10 suppressed parent | `delete_source` suppression → apply `NOT_FOUND_OR_UNAUTHORIZED`; branch stays `live`, no restore | yes |
| d10 purged parent | `execute_purge` tombstones the branch (`erased`); apply refused typed; revisions `[active, erased]` carry no proposed text; no apply receipt | yes |
| held → rebase | `kernel.invalidate` + `note_invalidation` flips branch `held` in the same tx; submit/apply `INVALID_TRANSITION`; `rebase` returns `live` | yes |
| abandon + diff | diff reports `ok`→`moved` honestly; abandoned branch cannot apply; audit record persists | yes |

- **D09 — met.** Branch ops are isolated overlays: live recall never saw
  the proposed archive until the reviewed apply ran it through the real
  `LifecycleMachine.apply` + `ReviewsRepo.resolve` inside one transaction,
  with an `operation_receipts` row making replay idempotent.
- **D10 — met.** Both propagation halves: suppression of a pinned parent
  refuses the apply (`purge_targets` fence), and full erasure tombstones
  the branch itself through the deletion closure — every revision doc
  becomes a digest-bound tombstone carrying no proposed text, and no
  receipt exists, so the branch can never resurrect purged bytes.

## I4 — environment- and counterexample-qualified transfer (D07, D08)

Corpus: 6 procedures compiled through the real producer path (every other
one carrying a recorded failure mode), 5 delivery scenarios per arm, paired
arms `positive_only` vs `failure_aware` in separate identical stores.
Full data: `eval/v45/out/i4_transfer_report.{json,md}`.

| scenario | arm | delivered | blocked | transfer_success | negative_transfer | task_success |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| in_env | positive_only | 6 | 0 | 6 | 0 | 6 |
| in_env | failure_aware | 6 | 0 | 6 | 0 | 6 |
| heldout | positive_only | 6 | 0 | 0 | **6** | 0 |
| heldout | failure_aware | 0 | 6 | 0 | **0** | 6 |
| unknown_env | positive_only | 6 | 0 | 6 | 0 | 6 |
| unknown_env | failure_aware | 6 | 0 | 6 | 0 | 6 |
| exposed_only | either | 6 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| self_report | either | 6 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

- **D07 — met.** Held-out environment (platform flip): positive-only reuse
  delivered all 6 cards and the reuse measurably failed → **6 negative
  transfers**. Failure-aware blocked all 6 deliveries (environment-mismatch
  verdict + counterexamples returned, no card, no exposure row) → **0
  negative transfers**, and every task still succeeded through the
  no-memory path (the V45-06.03 case where no-memory wins and unqualified
  reuse loses). The in-env success side is preserved identically on both
  arms (6/6 transfer success each — V45-06.04's "without erasing the
  success-side gain").
- **D08 — met.** `transfer_success()` requires a `procedure_exposures`
  delivery row *and* a host-attested positive checker receipt resolved
  through `episodes_v3.envelope_outcome`. Exposure with no outcome
  envelope: **6/6 not counted**. Delivery with an agent-reported
  "success" envelope: **6/6 not counted** (resolved `attested_outcome`
  stays `none`).
- Qualification surface measured: 30 failure-aware deliveries carried
  counterexamples with **540 evidence refs** verbatim from the compiler's
  `failure_modes_json`; unknown-verdict deliveries report `qualified`
  loudly, never a wildcard pass; blocked deliveries record no exposure
  (denominators stay honest).
- **Declared, not measured:** the host's ground truth (held-out reuse
  fails, no-memory succeeds) is a scenario declaration in
  `eval/v45/i4_transfer.py::GROUND_TRUTH`, printed in the report.

## I6 — utility-budgeted refresh vs periodic full reflection (D11, D12)

Corpus: 12 aggregation slots × 2 claims, 6 cycles per arm, 3 corrections
per change period, three paired stores (periodic+reflect, periodic
consolidate-only, budgeted). Budgeted arm: `regions_per_job=3`,
steady-state `max_jobs=1` emitted job per cycle.
Full data: `eval/v45/out/i6_refresh_report.{json,md}`.

| metric | periodic (+reflect) | periodic (consolidate-only) | budgeted | savings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| claims processed | 288 | 144 | **50** | **82.6%** / **65.3%** |
| bytes processed | 1740 | — | **297** | **82.9%** |
| jobs emitted | 6 | 6 | 9 | −50% (finer-grained) |
| work items (regions) | 6 | 6 | 25 | — |

- **D11 — met.** At the matched freshness floor (both arms end the window
  at **0 stale answers** — live observations citing closed claim revisions
  — and every steady-state cycle sat at the floor), the budgeted arm
  processed **82.6% fewer claim inputs** than periodic-with-reflection and
  **65.3% fewer** than a consolidate-only periodic — far above the 20%
  threshold. Jobs are honestly reported *up* (9 vs 6): targeted work is
  finer-grained; region packing (`regions_per_job`) bounds the overhead.
- **D12 — met.** The probe cycle ran `max_jobs=0` with an explicit owner
  request: owner work **scheduled 1 / deferred 0** while all 3 ordinary
  candidates deferred and reported their deferral; owner cost counted in
  spend (1 priority job). The held watermark re-surfaced the deferred
  regions and the catch-up cycle restored the floor (0 stale).
- Deferral is never silent: `refresh_pass` events record watermark
  before/after, per-candidate utility + reasons, scheduled/deferred lists,
  and measured spend before any job runs.

## Implementation

New production modules (additive; no changes to kernel, store resolver,
grant evaluator, trust model, or packer):

- `verbatim/repair.py` — `impact_closure`, `plan_repair`, `persist_plan`,
  `load_plan`, `invalidate_dependents`, `mark_held`, `apply_repair`,
  `full_rebuild`, `register_producer`. Composition layer over the same
  `derivations` + `dependency_edges` + side-walker graphs the kernel and
  deletion engine walk; digest-bound `repair_plan/v1` objects; apply-time
  closure re-walk + `CONTEXT_INCOMPLETE` fences; dispatches to the real
  `consolidate_windowed`, `refresh_scene`, `Synthesizer.compose`, and
  `ProfileService.refresh` producers.
- `verbatim/branches.py` — `BranchService` (`create`/`get`/`list`/
  `add_op`/`submit`/`apply`/`abandon`/`rebase`/`diff`/

  `dependency_edges` + `derivations` dual-write at create/add_op,
  `ReviewsRepo.create` expected-versions fencing, `LifecycleMachine.apply`

  (V45-07.04), tombstone-on-purge (V45-12.03).

## Remaining gaps

- **Disposition rows stay `defer`/`unmeasured`** — deliberately. The
  harness-scale paired ablations satisfy D03–D12 but not §16's
  default-routing bar (host qualification, statistical superiority,
  independent reproduction — §17). `test_d24_no_market_beating_claim`
  pins this.
- **I2 corpus scale** — 16 slots × 1 claim; deep chains (multi-level
  transitive dependents), procedure/trajectory dependents, and
  profile-entry refresh are exercised by unit tests but not in the
  paired locality numbers. The v2 `suppress()` path does not hold
  registry dependents itself — the apply fence (`purge_targets`) covers
  the window, and the durable held/erased marks land through
  `execute_purge`'s closure engine.
- **I5 scope** — single-scope fixtures; multi-op branches and rebase
  dead-op accounting are covered, but concurrent branch operations and
  retention-expiry sweep behavior are unmeasured at harness scale.
- **I4 host ground truth is declared, not learned** — the held-out
  failure outcome is a scenario constant; a real held-out repository
  corpus (V45-06.03's full form) with genuine checker execution remains
  open.
- **I6 corpus scale** — 12 slots × 2 claims; demand/readiness signals
  exist (`influence`, `readiness_obligations`) but the harness exercises
  them lightly. Larger-window cost curves are not yet published.
- **No live delivery wiring** — `deliver_procedure` is invoked directly;
  no retrieval lane calls it yet (intentional: transfer is advisory and
  exposure semantics must not inflate reuse stats).
- Job-count overhead on the budgeted arm (9 vs 6) is disclosed, not
  optimized away — `regions_per_job` is the knob; deeper batching (e.g.
  adaptive pack sizing) is unmeasured.
